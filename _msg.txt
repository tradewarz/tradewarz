Copy trading, part 1: the copy section of a strategy and the signal message

A bot can follow up to five wallets on its chain (strategy.copy): a buy by one of
them is a signal the tab may act on with its own size, exits and guardrails; sells
can be copied as the same share of the position. Addresses are checked against the
chain and must not repeat. The hub->tab stream gains a `signal` message (sent only
to the following account) and a `copy` candidate source for tokens first seen in a
followed wallet. Described in words like every other rule; tests cover the schema.
